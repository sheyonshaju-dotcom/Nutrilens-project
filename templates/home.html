Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about NutriLens and how it works.

How does NutriLens analyze food?

We combine AI image recognition with a comprehensive nutrient database to estimate calories, macros and portion sizes accurately.

How accurate is the scanner?

Accuracy improves with clear images and common foods. We continually train our AI models on regional dishes to improve precision.

Can I use NutriLens for Indian & regional foods?

Yes — our database includes many regional recipes and we support manual corrections for uncommon dishes to ensure accuracy.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is encrypted and not sold to advertisers. You control what you share with dietitians and other users.
